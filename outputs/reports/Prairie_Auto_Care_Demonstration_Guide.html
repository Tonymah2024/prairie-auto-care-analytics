Prairie Auto Care Group is a FICTIONAL multi-location Calgary collision repair and car care business. Its business model (three Calgary locations with distinct roles, published hours, service menu, OEM-certified collision work, direct billing of insurers) is inspired by a public Calgary auto care group's website (retrieved 2026-10-09); every repair order, cost, technician, insurer, customer and financial figure is synthetic. No relationship with or endorsement by any real company is implied.

Before the meeting

  • Open prairie-auto-care-bi.streamlit.app (or run locally: streamlit run dashboard/app.py).
  • Say clearly: fictional business, synthetic data, inspired only by the public business model of a Calgary auto care group.
